# Open-request reinforcements

The default is a general-purpose air reinforcement mix, not a claim of optimal defense against every army.

- 10 spaces: 2 Balloons
- 15: Baby Dragon + Balloon
- 20: Dragon
- 25: Dragon + Balloon
- 30: Electro Dragon
- 35: Electro Dragon + Balloon
- 40: 2 Dragons
- 45: 2 Dragons + Balloon
- 50: Electro Dragon + Dragon
- 55: Electro Dragon + Dragon + Balloon

These cover the Clan Castle troop capacities currently documented from level 1 through 14. The request's observed remaining capacity takes precedence over Town Hall assumptions. Troop-level restrictions and native eligibility still apply.

Sources checked October 28, 2026: [Clan Castle capacity reference](https://clashofclans.fandom.com/wiki/Clan_Castle), [current capacity table](https://coc.6oh.cn/en/shuju344.html), and [Supercell donation restrictions](https://supercell.com/en/games/clashofclans/blog/news/clan-castle-troop-donation-changes-2/).

Open and hybrid requests require identified troops. Unknown icons are skipped. Barbarian, Wall Breaker, Goblin, and their variants are excluded. Archers may fill a verified leftover gap of up to three spaces. Approved fallback troops are Baby Dragons, Balloons, Wizards, and Minions. Missing units or partially filled castles use a housing-budget plan; unknown capacity uses one substantial eligible troop per survey and never uses Archers as a guessed filler. Specific requests retain their explicit troop choices.

The bot surveys the troop pages before selecting its mix, then rereads eligibility, panel geometry, and elixir mode before each tap. The native game controls enforce donor limits and remaining capacity. A sent tap is not proof of delivery; concurrent donors may complete a request first.
